Verse Venture Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Verse Venture is a quiet word puzzle game for iPhone that walks through the Bible one story at a time. It is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This page explains, in plain words, what information the app uses, where it goes and what choices you have.

The short version

  • You never create an account, and we never ask for your name or email address.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your iPhone and, when iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud storage. We cannot read them.
  • If you buy something, Apple takes the payment and a service called RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase under a coded ID, so the app can open what you bought.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking.
  • We do not sell your information.

No accounts

You do not create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address or phone number. The app does not ask for them. It does not read your contacts or your photos, and it does not use your camera, microphone or location.

Your progress, coins and settings

The app saves your game on your iPhone. This includes the stages you have finished, the words you have found, the verses and puzzle pictures you have collected, your daily puzzle progress, your coin balance and a record of the coin purchases already added to it, and your settings for sound, haptics and tap to spell.

If iCloud is turned on for Verse Venture, the same saved game is also kept in your own private iCloud storage so that it follows you to your other devices and comes back if you reinstall the app. That copy lives in your iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. It is private to you. We cannot see it, read it or change it.

If iCloud is turned off for the app, your saved game stays on that iPhone only.

Purchases

Genesis is free. Other books can be opened with an optional subscription or, where it is offered, a one-time Lifetime unlock. You can also buy packs of coins, which are only used for hints.

Apple processes every payment. We never see or store your card or other payment details, and we do not receive your name, email address or Apple Account from Apple.

To know what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases. RevenueCat processes:

  • A coded ID. This is the iCloud user ID that Apple creates for you in this app. It is a string of letters and numbers that does not contain your name, email address or Apple Account, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. It lets your purchases follow you to your other devices.
  • Your purchase records. What you bought in the app, when, the price and currency, the App Store country, and whether a subscription is active, renewing, cancelled or refunded.
  • Basic technical details. Such as the app version, iOS version, device model, language and region settings, and an identifier Apple gives the app on your device. Your IP address is used to reach the service, as with any internet connection.

This information is used only to open what you have bought, to restore it on your devices, to add coins you have paid for, and to keep our sales records. Because the coded ID comes from iCloud, purchases and restoring purchases need iCloud to be turned on for the app.

You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.

App updates

New books and small fixes can be delivered to the app over the internet. When the app opens it asks an update service run by Expo whether a newer version of the game's content is available. That request includes basic technical details: the app version, the version of the content you have now, and a random ID for this installation of the app that Expo uses to count installs. Your IP address is used to deliver the answer. The request does not include your name, your progress or your purchases.

Sharing a verse or a finished book

When you choose to share a verse or the picture of a finished book, the app makes an image on your iPhone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you send it. We do not receive a copy and we do not know that you shared it.

If you choose to save the image to your photo library, your iPhone adds that one picture and may ask your permission first. This only lets the app add the picture. The app cannot see, read or change the photos you already have.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Verse Venture has no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not build a profile of you. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

If you have chosen on your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and usage totals that do not identify you. You can change this in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you describe, and we keep the message only as long as that takes or as the law requires.

This website

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit. The page itself uses no cookies and no tracking.

Who handles information for us

The only companies involved are the ones named above, each for its own task:

  • Apple: the App Store, payments and your iCloud storage.
  • RevenueCat: records of in-app purchases.
  • Expo: delivery of app updates.
  • GitHub: hosting of this page.
  • Our email provider: messages you send us.

These companies may process information in the United States and other countries. We do not give information to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your iPhone. Reset progress in the app's Settings clears your stages, verses and puzzle pictures, on this iPhone and, when iCloud is on, on your other devices. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on that iPhone.
  • In your iCloud. The iCloud copy stays until you remove it. You can delete it in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your storage, and choose Verse Venture.
  • Purchase records. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as the app is offered, and we keep sales records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete the purchase record held under your coded ID by writing to the address below. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for a detail that helps us find it, such as the order number on your Apple receipt. Apple keeps its own record of your purchases under its own policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, the law may give you the right to ask what information is held about you, to receive a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. To use any of these rights, write to the address below. We will answer within the time the law allows and we will never treat you differently for asking. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Where the law asks for a reason for using information, ours are these: to provide what you asked for (your purchases and your saved game), to keep the app working and up to date, and to meet legal duties such as keeping sales records.

Security

The app keeps as little information as it can. What does leave your iPhone travels over encrypted connections to the companies named above. No system can be promised to be perfectly secure, but there is very little here to protect.

Children

Verse Venture is a general audience game and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app never asks anyone for a name, email address or other contact detail, and purchases go through the Apple Account on the device, where a parent can require approval. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.
