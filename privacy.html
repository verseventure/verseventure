Verse Venture Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-14

Verse Venture is a quiet word puzzle game that walks through the Bible one story at a time. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with your information.

No accounts

You do not create an account, sign in, or give us your name or email address. We do not ask for them.

Your progress stays on your device

Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We do not receive them and we cannot see them. If you delete the app, that saved data is removed with it.

Subscriptions

Some books are opened with an optional subscription. To provide it, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that manages subscriptions. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user id and your purchase records so the app can tell whether your subscription is active and can restore it on your device. This id is not linked to your name or email address by us.

Apple processes the payment. We never see or store your card or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Verse Venture has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your information for advertising, and the app does not follow you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to verseventure@pm.me.
